Connection problems?

If joining fails with "Negotiation of connection failed", the two browsers can't reach each other directly (same router, VPN, strict firewall or a privacy browser). Try Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox first. A TURN relay server also fixes it; free ones exist (for example from Metered or Cloudflare), paste what they give you. One player filling this in is usually enough.

The game

Four Amys, one arena, no zombies. Walk around, shoot the others and stay alive. Every hit costs a heart, every player has five, and the first Amy to score 10 frags wins the round.

Everybody plays Amy from Virus LQP-79, so every Amy wears her player number above her head. Yours is the white one. A little bar under each Amy shows how many hearts she has left.
